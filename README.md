Chocolate Company Sales Analysis – Data Analytics Project

This project focuses on analyzing a chocolate company's sales data to identify sales trends, evaluate business performance, and generate meaningful insights through data analytics.

The analysis explores key business metrics such as total revenue, sales quantity, product performance, regional sales, and salesperson performance. Interactive visualizations and dashboards help present the findings in an easy-to-understand format and support data-driven business decisions.

Objectives

Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.

Identify top-performing chocolate products.

Compare sales across different regions.

Evaluate individual salesperson performance.

Identify sales trends and patterns.

Generate actionable business insights.

Tools & Technologies

Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation.

Microsoft Excel – Data preparation and analysis.

Key Outcomes

Identified high-performing products and sales regions.

Evaluated revenue and sales performance.

Presented business insights through interactive dashboards.

Supported better understanding of sales trends and business performance.

Project Type

Data Analytics | Business Intelligence | Sales Performance Analysis
